    How to read it

    ready: nothing it waits for is open
    blocked: waits for an open issue or epic
    done
    milestone: its epics' issues follow below the bar, down to the next; ordered by due date
    Boxes are epics, one per milestone they have issues in; a broad band joins the pieces of one epic. Arrows run from a blocker to what waits for it. Top to bottom is the order, without a time scale.
    Epics only: a row per epic, a column per milestone; each cell is the epic's issues in that milestone, its bar the share done. “after” names the epics it waits for.
    Drag cards and boxes to explore; nothing is saved. Scroll to zoom, drag the background to move, Fit to see it all; click a card to open its issue.
