      How to read it

      ready: nothing it waits for is open
      blocked: waits for an open issue or epic
      done
      milestone: its epics' issues follow below the bar, down to the next; ordered by due date
      Boxes are epics, one per milestone they have issues in; a broad band joins the pieces of one epic. Arrows run from a blocker to what waits for it. Top to bottom is the order, without a time scale.
      Without epic: open issues under no epic, in their milestone, in a box right of the epics.
      Epics only: the same roadmap with the issues hidden; each box shows its epic and how much of it is done in that milestone.
      Drag cards and boxes to explore; nothing is saved. Scroll to zoom, drag the background to move, Fit to see it all; click an issue, epic or milestone for its details (cmd-click opens it on GitHub).
