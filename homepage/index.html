Games that show how AI works

A place where young people play games run by AI, invent their own, and find out along the way what AI can do and how it does it.

Play, invent, understand

AI is part of young people's everyday lives. ChatGameLab turns it into something to explore and question, not just to use.

🎮

Play

Step into a story, decide what your character does, and see how the world answers. With pictures and voices, if you like.

✏️

Invent

Make up your own game: a world, a story, a look of its own. Then share it with friends.

🔍

Understand

Every game lets you look behind the scenes: what the AI is asked, what it answers, and why the story turns out the way it does.

For groups and workshops

Schools, youth centres, libraries and other organisations use ChatGameLab in their workshops, to talk about AI by playing with it.

  • Easy to join. Players join a workshop with a link. They need no account.
  • Safe for young players. Every game follows rules for the players' age group.
  • Made for learning. Seeing how a game works is part of every game, so it is easy to start a conversation about AI.

An open project

ChatGameLab is open source. Organisations use it with their own groups, anyone can follow how it grows, and everyone is welcome to take part.
